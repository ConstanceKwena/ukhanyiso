# Postgraduate bursary research, 24 October 2026 (Nana)

The six funds below were checked against the funders' own pages where we could find them, and against university notices and bursary listing sites where we could not. Data is in `postgrad-bursaries-2027.json`. Nothing here was added to `bursaries.json`: the bursary tests expect exactly five bursaries, each with a closing date.

## Corrections to the list we started from

1. **NRF.** The 65% average is confirmed by several universities quoting the NRF call. The age limits (28, 30, 32) are **not a cut-off when you apply**: universities say there is no age restriction at the application stage, and limits apply when awards are allocated. Applications go **through your university**, whose internal closing date is earlier than the NRF date. The first-time master's and doctoral calls have closed.
2. **UNISA.** It is for students registered at UNISA, not for any postgraduate student. New honours applicants are UNISA graduates with a 60% average over their whole undergraduate qualification. Applications for 2027 run from 21 September to 30 November 2026. The research-allowance figures in our list were ambiguous and are **not** confirmed, so they are left out.
3. **IDC.** **Closed on 30 September 2026** (IDC's own page). It is aimed at unemployed South Africans aged 26 or younger, mainly for a first undergraduate qualification. Postgraduate funding is limited to selected honours fields (law, mathematics, statistics, actuarial science, accounting CTA).
4. **SANSA.** **Closed on 30 September 2026.** The 65% average is on SANSA's page. The annual caps of R150,000, R160,000 and R170,000 match several sources.
5. **Canon Collins.** **Closed on 14 August 2026.** Awards are **R20,000 to R100,000**, so it is not a full-cost scholarship. The deadline is 4:00 pm GMT, which is 18:00 South African time (some sites wrongly say 16:00). It is for citizens of eligible Southern African countries, with at least two years of social justice involvement.
6. **ISFAP postgraduate.** Not verified. The only evidence found is that honours applicants needing an ISFAP assessment had to apply to their university by 24 August 2026. No ISFAP page was opened.
