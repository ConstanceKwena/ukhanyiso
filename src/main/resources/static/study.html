When you are available

For each study day, enter your school or class hours and a study window that does not overlap. Times are editable to fit your own timetable.

Your weekly timetable

Study blocks are scheduled only inside your chosen study window, outside the school/class hours you entered. A short break separates longer study periods. The planner may use fewer minutes when the remaining time cannot fit another short session and break.

Study links
