How to read likelihood labels

  • Likely: you meet the checked requirements and are at least 3 APS points above the minimum.
  • Possible: you meet the checked requirements but are less than 3 APS points above the minimum.
  • Unlikely: one or more requirements are not met. Near-miss cards explain the gap and next steps.

These labels are guides, not admission promises. Confirm requirements with the institution.
