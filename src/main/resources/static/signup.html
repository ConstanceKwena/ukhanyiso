Create your Ukhanyiso account

Sign up with your email address and a unique password. You will add your personal details on the next page. Do not reuse a password from another account. Do not enter an ID or exam number.

Your email is your username. You will log in with it, and it is also used if you need to reset your password.

Use 12–128 characters. A unique passphrase is okay; no special symbol is required. Do not reuse another account’s password.

Locally, your account and checklist progress are saved in a database file on this computer; hosted accounts persist only when the app is configured with a persistent database. Do not enter an ID or exam number.
